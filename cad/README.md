# Robinson – acrylic plates

All plates are 4 mm acrylic. Rods: M4 threaded rods through the Ø 4.3 mm holes.

| File | Size | What |
|---|---|---|
| `top-plate-eyes-pi5-pca9685.stl` | 160 × 210 mm | Top plate: Will Cogley's EyeMech ε3.2 at the front (4 × Ø 4.5 for M4, 47.0 × 57.2 mm), Raspberry Pi 5 behind it on the left (4 × Ø 2.7 for M2.5, 58 × 49 mm, USB towards the edge), HW-170 / PCA9685 servo board on the right (4 slots 3.2 mm, ~19 × 56 mm, ±1.5 mm). **In mm.** |
| `onshape-robinson.stl` | 160 × 210 mm | Original top plate from Onshape (with the two side notches). |
| `onshape-part-1.stl` | 160 × 210 mm | Plate, 8 rod holes (pairs in the corners). |
| `onshape-part-2.stl` | 160 × 120 mm | Plate with a trapezoid cutout on one edge, 6 holes. |
| `onshape-part-3.stl` | 160 × 210 mm | Plate, 4 corner holes + 3 mounting holes. |

**Units:** the `onshape-*` files are exported at 1:100 (2.1 units = 210 mm). Scale them by
100 before slicing or laser cutting – FreeCAD and most slicers read STL as millimetres.

Before cutting the top plate, check the hole spacing of your HW-170 (the clones vary).
