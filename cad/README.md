# Robinson – acrylic plates

All plates are 4 mm acrylic, all files in **millimetres**. Rods: M4 threaded rods through
the Ø 4.3 mm holes.

| File | Size | What |
|---|---|---|
| `top-plate-eyes-pi5-pca9685.stl` | 160 × 210 mm | Top plate: Will Cogley's EyeMech ε3.2 at the front (4 × Ø 4.5 for M4, 47.0 × 57.2 mm), Raspberry Pi 5 behind it on the left (4 × Ø 2.7 for M2.5, 58 × 49 mm, USB towards the edge), HW-170 / PCA9685 servo board on the right (4 slots 3.2 mm, ~19 × 56 mm, ±1.5 mm). 10 rod holes. |
| `top-plate-original-side-notches.stl` | 160 × 210 mm | Original top plate from Onshape, two notches in the long sides, 10 rod holes. |
| `plate-160x210-rod-holes.stl` | 160 × 210 mm | Plain plate, 8 rod holes (pairs in the corners). Onshape "Part 1". |
| `plate-160x120-trapezoid-cutout.stl` | 160 × 120 mm | Plate with a trapezoid cutout on one long edge, 6 holes. Onshape "Part 2". |
| `plate-160x210-board-holes.stl` | 160 × 210 mm | Plate with 4 corner holes + 3 mounting holes (all Ø 4.3). Onshape "Part 3". |

The Onshape exports came at 1:100 (2.1 units = 210 mm) and were scaled to mm here. For
new exports choose *Units: Millimeter* in Onshape's STL export dialog.

Before cutting the top plate, check the hole spacing of your HW-170 (the clones vary).
